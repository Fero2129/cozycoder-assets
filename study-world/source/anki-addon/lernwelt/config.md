**Study World / Lernwelt** – most settings (language, grace period, app lock, allowed apps, book colours) are in the add-on itself: open it and click the settings book at the bottom right.

- `show_focus_bar`: show a slim bar with your growing book above the cards during a focus session.
- `toolbar_button`: show a button in Anki's top toolbar (otherwise only in the Tools menu, Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+L).

*Deutsch:* Die meisten Einstellungen findest du in der Lernwelt selbst (Einstellungsbuch unten rechts). `show_focus_bar` = Fokus-Leiste über den Karten, `toolbar_button` = Knopf in der oberen Leiste.
